# VYNE — Canonical iPhone UI/UX

## Navigation
Tabs: Home, Cleanup, Smart Albums. Secondary screens: Photo Details, Learning, Storage, Privacy & Settings.

## Home
VYNE wordmark, photos analyzed, progressive scan status, estimated cleanup space, duplicates/screenshots/blur summaries, prominent “Review Cleanup” CTA, smart album previews. Never display illustrative metrics as real user data.

## Cleanup Review
Large photo with filmstrip of alternatives, best-shot suggestion, concise rationale, “Why?”, Keep, Skip, and explicit Approve Deletion. Group by Duplicates, Similar, Screenshots, Blurry, Other. Require clear confirmation before native PhotoKit deletion.

## Smart Albums
Photo-first category grid and filters: People, Places, Things, Events. Examples: Family, Sports, Trips, Work & Projects, Pets, Screenshots. Many-to-many membership; correct tags and create personal collections. Differentiate app collections from optional Apple Photos sync.

## Photo Details
Edge-to-edge image, contextual tags, capture metadata, event associations, explainable AI insights, protection rationale, and correction affordances. Avoid unsupported claims of emotional importance.

## Automatic Organization
Show photos recently indexed and assigned. Changes observed while app runs; background best-effort and catch-up on next launch. Do not promise instant push notifications after every capture.

## Learning
Readable summaries of learned preferences and examples; correction and Reset Learning. No invented confidence metrics.

## Storage
Clearly labeled reclaimable-space estimates, no double-counting, explanation of Recently Deleted behavior.

## Privacy
PhotoKit access, local analysis, optional cloud opt-in, optional Apple Photos album sync, learning reset and local index deletion.

## Interaction and accessibility
Midnight-teal palette per BRAND.md; bright teal reserved for action. Photo-first, restrained surfaces, large touch targets, VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, reduced-motion, permission-denied and interrupted-scan states. Destructive action must never be the default emphasized action.

## First interactive milestone
Navigable Home, Cleanup, Albums, Photo Details and Settings with labeled fixture data; replace fixtures only after native indexing works.
