# VYNE — Canonical Brand Kit
**Visual direction approved. Name and final vector logo provisional.**

## Identity
Working name: VYNE. Tagline: **Your life in focus.** Positioning: **Photos that understand you.**
Personality: premium, calm, intelligent, personal, protective, effortless.

## Logo
Fluid abstract V / connected ribbon with sea-glass luminance. Must remain legible as a simple flat monochrome icon. Current artwork is exploratory, not final production vector.

## Typography and surfaces
Restrained modern sans-serif; start with iOS system fonts. Spacious, photo-first layouts; soft layered midnight-teal panels, subtle translucency and rounded corners. Keep brighter teal rare and meaningful. Avoid neon cyberpunk, busy glass effects, dense charts, and generic cleaner-app visuals.

## Motion
Gentle transitions, grouping and review feedback; honor reduced motion.

## Copy
Use “Review cleanup”, “Keep the best shot”, “Likely duplicate”, “Why this suggestion?”. Never call sentimental photos garbage.

## Assets
Reference mockups are design inspiration, not production-ready assets. No font files or unlicensed images are included. Trademark, domain and App Store checks remain outstanding.
